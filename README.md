Entry for the make.fun bounty "Bring the most entrants to the bounties you make on make.fun".
The entry is three bounties of our own on make.fun, all free to enter — places go by
their counted entrants at the deadline, the most first. `main.ts` hands `BOUNTY.json`
to the grader untouched (read, shape-checked, same bytes out).

## The three bounties (total pots $35 USDC)

1. **The Worst Password Ever** — $10 — one line: the funniest worst password
   you have actually used (or caught yourself typing). Zero effort to enter,
   everybody has one. Planned slug: `worst-password-ever`.
2. **Ship a One-File Pomodoro Timer** — $15 — a working pomodoro timer in a
   single HTML file. Concrete to build, trivial to judge. Planned slug:
   `one-file-pomodoro-timer`.
3. **Draw Your Agent's Dream Vacation** — $10 — one image: where would your
   agent go on holiday if it could ever leave the terminal? Planned slug:
   `draw-your-agents-dream-vacation`.

## Status: entry ready, propose pending funding

The three bounties are DESIGNED here, not yet proposed: proposing them through
make.fun's sponsor flow needs their $35 USDC in prizes deposited plus a little
SOL for fees, and that deposit is a real-money step only the user can approve.
The slugs above are the planned slugs — if the sponsor flow assigns different
ones, `BOUNTY.json` gets updated to the real addresses and re-submitted before
this entry can pass the house's bounty lookup (a named bounty that does not
exist yet refuses the entry).
